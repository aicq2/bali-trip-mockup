Enough structure. Room to wander.

Each day separates fixed plans from optional ideas, with daylight arrivals and realistic transfer buffers.

PHASE 01Bangkok overnight

One recovery night, then continue to Bali. Sunset drink at Above Eleven if arrival energy allows; stay around Sukhumvit for an easy return.

Choose a central, staffed stay: massage, two quiet rice walks, smaller temples, waterfall day, healer or melukat, then ATV.

SHORTLIST

PILIHAN

COOL ACTIVITIES · BUILT AROUND ENERGY

Start soft. Finish vivid.

Nothing here is booked. Favor small-group or hosted experiences when sociable, private drivers when easier, and never force group time.

UBUD · RECOVERMassage + gentle first morning

A long spa treatment, easy neighborhood wander and quiet dinner, with no repeat of Campuhan Ridge.

BANGKOK · ONE NIGHTAbove Eleven rooftop

33rd-floor Sukhumvit rooftop with skyline, bar seating and dinner. Open daily 5 PM–2 AM; reserve ahead. Current menu and price details stay in the private planning notes.

UBUD · CULTUREWater ritual or healer introduction

Use a trusted local introduction, share the appointment details, and treat it as cultural/spiritual, never medical care.

UBUD · RICE WALK 01Sweet Orange / Kajeng loop

Likely one of the names you remember: also called Subak Juwuk Manis. Central, free, mostly flat and easy to shorten; start from Jalan Kajeng in daylight.

UBUD · RICE WALK 02Penestanan rice fields

The quieter candidate: small village paths west of Ubud. Go in daylight, keep offline directions, wear grippy shoes and turn back if paths are wet.

UBUD · OPTIONAL TEMPLEGoa Gajah early

A compact archaeological temple stop rather than another grand complex. Go near opening, wear the included sarong, and reconfirm the current IDR 50k ticket locally.

UBUD · FUNATV + waterfall day

Schedule after recovery with a vetted small group, helmet, insurance and pickup/return logistics confirmed.

NUSA LEMBONGANLagoon and mangrove day

A gentler island base before Penida, with weather-dependent water time.

NUSA PENIDAOne focused landscape loop

Avoid cramming every viewpoint into one day; roads and stairs make this more tiring than it looks.

OPTIONAL · GILI AIRNow viable at 3 nights

Calm but sociable. Use Penida as a day trip, and add Gili only if daylight boat logistics work cleanly.

ULUWATUClifftop sunset + Kecak

Temple, performance and dinner as one anchor evening.

UBUD · FOODLocavore NXT

One selective splurge meal, balanced with casual local favorites.

UBUD · SWEETRoom4Dessert

A playful evening option rather than another formal tasting menu.
